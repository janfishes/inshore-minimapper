Import a track or a file GPX off the plotter card, or a CSV
The ACTIVE LOG is the one worth exporting. On the ECHOMAP, saving a track throws away the timestamps and the depths and keeps only the shape — every usable sounding lives in ACTIVE LOG, and that is a rolling buffer, so export before it wraps.

Garmin writes metres even when the screen says feet. The gpxx schema defines depth in metres and the unit converts on the way out. This converts once, and says so on every import — if a number looks four times too shallow, that is the trap you are looking at.
Spots what each one really holds at dead low
A spread over 3 ft is not one depth — it is a slope, and no single number describes it. Either the pass crossed the edge of a hole or a bar, or a reading was taken off bottom.
Export & housekeeping CSV out, and where this lives
Soundings are kept on this device and nowhere else. Take the CSV or the backup off it now and then — a cleared browser takes the lot. Re-reduce is worth running after you calibrate a lag on the tide board: every sounding already taken improves, because what is stored is the raw reading and the minute, not just the answer.
